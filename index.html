Mindful Living

Life & Style Playbook

Simple rules and daily habits to keep your closet minimal, your outfits cohesive, and your days organized.

01

The 333 Capsule Rule

Select 3 tops, 3 bottoms, and 3 pairs of shoes. By mixing and matching these 9 items, you can create up to 27 distinct outfits—enough to cover almost a full month of stylish variations without closet clutter.

Style Strategy
02

Color Sandwich Technique

Match the color tone of your top (or headwear) with your shoes, sandwiching a contrasting or neutral bottom piece in between. This visually balances your silhouette instantly with zero effort.

Color Harmony
03

Skin Care Sandwiching

Apply lighter hydrating serums on damp skin before sealing them with lightweight moisturizer and SPF. Double cleansing in the evening removes sunscreen residue and ensures skin clarity.

Skincare Routine
04

The 2-Minute Micro Reset

If a home or organizational task takes less than two minutes (putting shoes away, hanging up a coat, making the bed), do it immediately. This prevents small tasks from building into mental overwhelm.
